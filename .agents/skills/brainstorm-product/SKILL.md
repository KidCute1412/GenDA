---
name: brainstorm-product
description: Brainstorm and prioritize SkillBridge product or workflow ideas without jumping prematurely into implementation.
---

Frame the user/problem and constraints first. Generate several distinct options, then compare impact, effort, risk, trust implications, and MVP fit. Separate assumptions from evidence. Recommend at most three experiments with measurable success criteria, then convert the selected idea into a goal, acceptance criteria, and likely vertical slice. Do not invent technical architecture unless asked; consult the domain docs when product behavior affects existing workflows.
